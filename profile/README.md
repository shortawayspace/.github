## 👋 Welcome to ShortAway
 
This is the private GitHub organization of [shortaway.com](https://shortaway.com/en).
 
ShortAway is a platform that connects homeowners with trusted local concierges who take care of their short-term rental: check-ins, cleaning, and guest care.
